Data reliability

Reliability reflects how large the margin of error is relative to the estimate (the coefficient of variation, CV -- Census Bureau convention): reliable CV < 15% · caution CV 15–30% · unreliable CV > 30%. Veteran-status cross-tabs (poverty, unemployment, income, education by Veteran status) land in caution/unreliable more often in smaller counties -- treat those numbers as directional, not precise.
